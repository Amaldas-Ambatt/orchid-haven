Care Secrets

How to Grow Orchids in Kerala's Climate

Kerala’s tropical warmth and natural humidity make orchids thrive if you follow these four simple principles perfected at Orchid Haven.

50% to 75% Shade Net

Avoid harsh midday direct sun. A green agro shade net (50% for Dendrobiums, 70% for Phalaenopsis) creates the optimal dappled light for continuous flower spikes.

  • Light lime-green leaves indicate high flower spike potential.
  • Deep dark green leaves mean more light is needed.

Charcoal & Coconut Husk

Never use garden soil or mud. Epiphytic orchids need their roots to breathe. Use washed coconut husk chunks and chunky horticultural charcoal in slotted terracotta or plastic pots.

  • Charcoal prevents fungal infections and root souring.
  • Coconut husk holds gentle moisture during dry months.

Monsoon & Summer Watering

In Kerala summer, water thoroughly early morning before 8:30 AM. In heavy monsoons, ensure rapid drainage so water never pools inside the crowns, preventing crown rot.

  • Water roots, avoid soaking open petals.
  • Keep plants in an airy space with cross-ventilation.

Feeding Cycles

Feed "weakly, weekly". Spray foliar NPK 19:19:19 (balanced) during growth phases, and rotate with high-potash bloom booster (NPK 13:27:27) to trigger massive flower spikes.

  • Always spray under the leaves in early morning.
  • Flush roots with plain water fortnightly.
