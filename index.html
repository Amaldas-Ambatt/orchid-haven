Care & Cultivation

How to Grow Orchids in Kerala's Climate

Kerala’s tropical warmth and natural humidity make it one of the finest places on Earth for orchids—provided you master four simple rules taught by our nursery over 15+ years.

50% to 75% Shade Net

Never place orchids in direct harsh tropical afternoon sun. A green agronet (50% for Vandas and Dendrobiums, 70% for Phalaenopsis) produces healthy emerald-lime leaves without sun scorch.

  • Dark green leaves mean too much shade.
  • Light yellowish-green is the ideal bloom color.

Charcoal & Coconut Husk

Orchids are epiphytes; never plant them in ordinary garden soil. Use chunky charcoal cubes and pre-washed coconut husk chips in perforated clay or plastic pots for maximum air circulation.

  • Charcoal prevents fungal infections.
  • Husk retains gentle moisture without waterlogging.

Monsoon & Summer Watering

In dry Kerala summers (Feb–May), water thoroughly early morning and mist in the evening. During heavy Kerala monsoons (June–August), ensure rain drains completely within minutes to protect roots.

  • Vandas love bare roots hanging in open air.
  • Apply preventive organic bio-fungicide in monsoon.

Fertilization Cycles

"Weakly, weekly" is the golden motto. Spray foliar NPK 19:19:19 during vegetative growth periods, and switch to high-phosphorus bloom booster (NPK 13:27:27) before the flowering season.

  • Always spray under the leaves before 9:00 AM.
  • Flush roots with plain water once every 2 weeks.
